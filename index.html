        Rules

        Whoever has posted the biggest bounty for a region sits at #1 for that region. Ties are broken by whoever paid first.

        • Bounties start at £5. There's no maximum.
        • Every bounty is its own entry — to reclaim #1 after someone outbids you, post a new, higher bounty.
        • Rankings update automatically as soon as a payment is confirmed.
        • Only list a coffeeshop you have the right to list — we may remove listings that are fake, abusive, illegal, or clearly not a genuine coffeeshop, without a refund.
