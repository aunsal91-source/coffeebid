      coffeebid.lol is a pay-to-rank leaderboard for Britain's coffeeshops. Whoever posts the biggest bounty sits at #1 as the nation's Most Wanted — outbid them and you take their spot, pushing them down the board.

      This is a satirical, fan-made demo inspired by outbid.lol. All bounties, rankings and stats live in your browser's local storage — nothing is sent anywhere, no real money changes hands, and no coffeeshop listed here is affiliated with or has endorsed this site.
